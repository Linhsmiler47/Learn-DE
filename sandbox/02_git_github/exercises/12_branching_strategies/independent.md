# Independent Exercise — Lesson 12: Branching Strategies & Collaborative Workflows

## Goal

Argue for a *different* strategy than the one this project uses, for a
scenario where it would genuinely fit better — proving you understand the
trade-offs, not just this project's choice.

## Task

Design a hypothetical scenario (three sentences is enough — a team size,
a release cadence, a support requirement) where **Git Flow** would be the
better choice than GitHub Flow, and a separate hypothetical scenario where
**trunk-based development** would be better than both. For each, explain
specifically what property of the scenario drives the choice — don't just
restate the lesson's generic descriptions.

## Constraints

- Your two scenarios must be genuinely different from each other and from
  this learning path's actual situation — the point is demonstrating the
  trade-off space, not restating "solo learner, one repo" a third time.

## Expected Behavior

Each scenario's justification should reference a *specific* property (team
size, release/support cadence, testing maturity) that makes that strategy
the right fit — not a vague "it's more organized" style answer.

## Validation Commands

N/A — this is a written reasoning exercise; the "validation" is whether
your own justification would survive someone else asking "why not the
other two strategies instead?"

## Evidence to Submit

In `notes/lesson_12_evidence.md`: both scenarios, your strategy choice
for each, and the specific property driving that choice — plus, for each,
one honest sentence about what that strategy would cost in that scenario.

## Do Not

- Do not just restate the lesson's comparison table without applying it
  to your own constructed scenarios.
